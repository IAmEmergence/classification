    BEHIND THE SCENES

    How does it work?

    Ever wondered how a few simple questions can turn into travel recommendations? Let's find out.

    01. The idea

    Everyone has different preferences when travelling. Some people love mountains, others prefer beaches. Some enjoy peaceful places, while others seek adventure.

    This website uses your answers to find destinations that match your preferences and budget.

    02. Your answers

    You answer eight questions about your travel preferences, including your budget, preferred climate, activities, terrain and more.

    These answers provide the information needed to make a recommendation.

    03. The journey of your answers

    1. Frontend: The website collects your answers.
    2. Backend: FastAPI receives and validates them.
    3. Data processing: Your answers are converted into numerical values that the model can understand.
    4. Machine learning: A trained PyTorch model evaluates the destinations.
    5. Results: The best affordable matches are sent back to the website.

    04. A little more technical

    The machine learning model is a neural network. It was trained using examples so that it could learn relationships between travel preferences and destinations.

    When you submit your answers, they are represented as a tensor containing 30 numerical features. The neural network processes this input and produces scores for the available destinations.

    05. Choosing your destinations

    The model's scores help rank destinations according to how well they match your answers.

    The system also checks your budget. Destinations exceeding it are excluded, and up to five affordable recommendations are returned.

    And that's how it works!

    From eight simple answers to personalised travel recommendations, several components work together behind the scenes.
